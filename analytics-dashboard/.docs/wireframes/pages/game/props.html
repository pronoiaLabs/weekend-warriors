Reading the board

notes

A soft line is a big Δ pointed at a leak: Shakir +13.8 into the 28th-ranked WR defense is the page's thesis. A negative Δ is an under lean, not a fade of the player. Lines move toward projections as the week ages, so the gaps here are what is left on Sunday morning. Rice carries a Q badge; his line already gave back a reception. The situation behind a prop lives one tab over in Situations, and the line's own path in Lines.
