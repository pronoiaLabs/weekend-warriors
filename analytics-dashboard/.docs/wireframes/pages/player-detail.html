Is his usage stable enough to trust the over at 86.5?
Mostly yes. Eight or more targets every week and a 24–33% target-share band, so the floor is real. The over is 5–2, and his share spikes exactly where CLE figures to force him: 3rd down and trailing script, 34% in each. I highlighted those two buckets in the usage strip — the sage cells on the right.
What's the caveat?
Script. Leading drops him to 25% share, and both unders (Wk 3, Wk 6) were his two lowest air-share weeks — when CIN sits on a lead the deep ball disappears. CIN is only −3.5 at CLE, so a competitive game keeps the trailing boost live.
