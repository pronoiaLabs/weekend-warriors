Where the week's lines opened, where they are now, and when they moved. Every point on a path is a real snapshot — the collapsed facts only keep an observation when something changed, so a flat line really was flat.

Book: DraftKings (FanDuel and BetMGM shown muted on the paths).

data — movers board 
mart:  APP.LINE_HISTORY  (fact_game_betting_odds_snapshot, collapsed)
route: GET /api/nfl/markets?season=2025&week=8&vendor=draftkings
grain: game_key × book × market × observed_at — a row means the line MOVED
       (consecutive identical observations are collapsed away upstream)
fields: game_key, book, market (spread|total|home_ml), open_value, latest_value,
        delta, move_count, spread_path[] (for the sparkline), last_moved_at
note:  open = first pre-kickoff snapshot; latest = most recent pre-kickoff row.
       games with no line for the chosen book keep one vendor-NULL row.

data — prop paths 
mart:  APP.PROP_LINE_HISTORY  (fact_player_prop_snapshot, collapsed)
route: GET /api/nfl/markets/{game_key}
grain: player × prop_type × book × observed_at, is_pre_kickoff = true
fields: player, team, prop_type, open_line, current_line, line_delta,
        over_odds_open, over_odds_now, book, line_path[]

       OR odds changed, so odds-only moves still snapshot (that is how the
       steam-vs-buyback row is detectable at all).

Why did the KC spread move?
KC went −1.5 → −2.5 and the move is Thursday-shaped: DraftKings stepped to −2.0 Thursday night and −2.5 Friday morning; FanDuel and BetMGM followed within hours. Anything moving against its odds?
One: Allen passing yards steamed 249.5 → 256.5 while the over's price eased −115 → −108 — the book raised the number and still got buyback. I flagged that row in the prop table below the chart.
