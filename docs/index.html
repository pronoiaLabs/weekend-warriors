Hear Me Out · a series on building unexpected things on Snowflake

You can run an entire football data platform inside Snowflake.

NFL and college football data, loaded on schedule, modeled, queryable in plain English, for fantasy football, betting analysis, and sports data tinkerers. Zero servers outside one Snowflake account.

§ I.

The Argument

five substitutions, no bench

Every piece of infrastructure a small data platform usually demands (a VPS, a cron box, a dbt runner, a chatbot backend, an alerting service) has a counterpart already sitting inside the warehouse.a The whole platform is the sum of five substitutions.

No. 1 Snowpark Container Services → your compute, no VPS
No. 2 Tasks → your scheduler, no cron box
No. 3 dbt runs INSIDE the warehouse → no runner to host
No. 4 Cortex Agents → ask your data questions, no chatbot to build
No. 5 Native webhook notifications → Slack alerts, no alerting stack

a "Inside" is meant literally: one Snowflake account holds the containers, the schedule, the models, the agents, and the alerting. Nothing else is deployed anywhere.
